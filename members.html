Admin only
Update the roster from CODM screenshots, fix UIDs and edit members. The password is checked by the clan server.
📸 Clan log screenshots

In CODM go to Clan → Member → Clan Log (or Member List) and screenshot it — scroll for more. Add the screenshots here: Claude reads them and suggests the joins and leaves. Nothing is saved until you tap Apply.

🪪 Profile screenshots — UIDs

Open a player's Player Profile (the screen showing UID:) and screenshot it. Claude reads the name and UID and matches the player to the roster, so you can fill in missing UIDs and pick up name changes.

✎ Edit by hand

Tap ✎ next to any member in the list to change their name, UID, slot, dates or notes. Character reference images are under 🖼.

📋 Paste a text log

Paste your full members log (the 📖 format). It replaces the whole roster, keeping reference images and old names for members it recognises by UID.
